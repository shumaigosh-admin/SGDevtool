Reset / Wipe
Destructive operations. Irreversible.
Delete One Customer

Removes selected customer from localStorage and Firebase.

Clear Activity Log

Wipes all activity log entries from localStorage.

⚠ Wipe All localStorage

Removes ALL SG keys from localStorage: customers, passports, activity, session, id_seq. Does not touch Firebase.

⚠ Wipe Firebase customers/

Deletes all records under customers/ in Firebase Realtime Database. meta/id_seq is NOT reset.

⚠ Wipe Firebase auditLogs/

Deletes ALL entries under auditLogs/ in Firebase. Useful for clearing test noise before a real deployment. Irreversible.

⚠ Wipe Firebase stamps_log/ + rewardClaims/

Deletes all per-customer stamp transaction logs and reward claim records. Does not affect customer records or stamp counts.

☢ FULL NUCLEAR WIPE

Wipes everything: localStorage + Firebase customers/ + resets id_seq to 0. Next registered account will be SG-000001. Completely irreversible.
